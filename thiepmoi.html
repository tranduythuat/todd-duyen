Exploring Da Nang

We'd be thrilled with your decision to join us in Đà Nẵng!

This beautiful coastal region holds a special place in our hearts. We fell in love with the warm, kind-hearted people, the incredible local food, and the relaxed yet vibrant rhythm of life here. That’s why we chose this place to celebrate our destination wedding — to share the magic we’ve discovered with the people we love most.

Flights & Arrival

Best Airports to Fly Into: Book a flight to Da Nang International Airport (DAD), the main airport in Da Nang City.

Popular Airlines: Major airlines like Vietnam Airlines, EVA Air, Hong Kong Express, Korean Air, and ANA offer comfortable flights with layovers in hubs like Taipei, Tokyo, Hong Kong, and Incheon.

Visa Requirements: Some countries are visa-exempt; you can check to see if your home country falls in this category. If not, you can apply for an eVisa online ahead of time.
